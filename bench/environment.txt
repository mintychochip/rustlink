rustlink benchmark record
=========================

Files
  results.jsonl   One row per cell (405). Every row is a verbatim line written by `sweep`, taken from
                  the sweep named in replicates.tsv (column chosen_run).
  results.md      The report, rendered from results.jsonl with `sweep --report-only`.
  replicates.tsv  Per cell: the median traversal time, the background load and the time lost on the
                  pinned CPU for each of the six sweeps, and which sweep results.jsonl uses. The
                  selection below can be re-derived from this file alone.

Machine and settings
  CPU          Intel Core Ultra 7 270K Plus, 24 CPUs, no SMT; sweeps pinned to cpu2 (the CPU with the
               highest cpuinfo_max_freq, 5.5 GHz). Caches of cpu2: L1d 48K, L2 3 MiB, L3 36 MiB shared.
  Kernel       7.1.10-200.fc44.x86_64
  Compiler     rustc 1.99.0-nightly (6f72b5dd5 2026-07-22), release profile (opt-level 3, fat LTO)
  Power        cpu2 scaling_governor powersave, energy_performance_preference balance_performance,
               intel_pstate no_turbo 0 (turbo on). Not changed.
  Binaries     Built from 327e31f. Commit 17589d9 changes only the report renderer, which is why the
               report header names that revision.
  Matrix       The default matrix: 405 cells, seed 1, 6 repetitions per cell (1 discarded warm-up and
               5 measured, inside one fresh process). No cell was skipped for memory.

Why six sweeps
  The machine is a desktop shared with other sessions that build and test in parallel (load average
  above 30 at times). Background load slows large cells measurably, so one sweep is not a trustworthy
  record. Six complete sweeps of the same matrix and seed were taken, and for each cell the sweep
  taken under the least interference is used.

  run  window (2026-10-23)  other CPUs busy   lost on cpu2   invocation
  1    05:56:41-06:06:20    1.68 cores        0.2 s          sweep (one invocation, whole matrix)
  2    06:08:01-06:18:41    5.46 cores        10.8 s         sweep (same)
  3    07:27:25-07:37:42    5.43 cores        1.9 s          sweep --workloads W, one per workload
  4    07:37:42-07:47:35    3.28 cores        4.6 s          (same, workload order rotated per run)
  5    07:47:35-07:58:02    2.47 cores        87.5 s         (same)
  6    07:58:02-08:08:25    4.31 cores        11.5 s         (same)
  "Other CPUs busy" is the mean number of busy CPUs other than cpu2, "lost" the time tasks waited for
  cpu2 while the benchmark ran (both sampled from /proc/stat and /proc/schedstat at 10 Hz by a monitor
  pinned to another CPU). The sweep drivers and the monitor were kept off cpu2 with taskset.

Selection rule for a done cell
  A sweep's measurement of a cell is eligible if
    * the other CPUs averaged fewer than 2.0 busy cores over the cell's time window padded by 0.5 s on
      each side, and
    * cpu2 lost at most max(20 ms, 2% of the cell's time) to other tasks.
  results.jsonl holds the eligible measurement with the lowest median traversal time (ties: the
  lower run number). 293 of the 296 done cells have at least one eligible measurement; the other 3
  (bst-random/usize at 2^22, blob-lookup/abs16 at 2^14 and blob-lookup/rel16 at 2^12) use the same
  rule with fewer than 3.0 busy cores. The load of the chosen measurements has a median of 1.43 busy
  cores and a maximum of 2.95. Unreachable cells are deterministic (they only report link distances);
  their rows come from the least loaded sweep.

What the load does (the reason for the rule)
  Median ratio of a measurement to the fastest measurement of the same cell, cells of 2^18 nodes or
  more, grouped by the load during the measurement (all six sweeps, cpu2 loss within the rule):
    other busy cores   <1.5    1.5-2   2-3     3-4     4-6     >=6
    all sizes          1.002   1.029   1.054   1.072   1.125   1.152
    2^20 only          1.000   1.057   1.112   1.162   1.246   1.146 (4 cells)
  Cells of 2^20 nodes (12 MiB of 12-byte nodes: bigger than the 3 MiB L2, inside the shared L3) are
  the most sensitive.

How far the eligible measurements of a cell agree (per cell max / min of the traversal medians;
cells with at least two eligible measurements)
    size       2^10   2^12   2^14   2^16   2^18   2^20   2^22   2^24
    median     1.105  1.035  1.019  1.025  1.026  1.076  1.035  1.039
    90th pct   1.497  1.163  1.062  1.046  1.075  1.170  1.084  1.044
  The in-process MAD in the report does not show this: it measures the five repetitions of one
  process, while the spread above includes different processes (different physical page layout) and
  different times. Cells of 2^12 nodes and fewer take microseconds per repetition and are only
  indicative; the other tables are good to a few percent, and about 8% at 2^20.

Reproduce
  cargo build --release --bins && target/release/sweep     (one sweep; keep the machine idle)
  Check the load of a sweep the way this record did: sample /proc/stat (busy jiffies of every CPU
  except the pinned one) and /proc/schedstat (the pinned CPU's rq_run_delay) while it runs. Repeat
  sweeps and keep the least disturbed measurement per cell if the machine cannot be idle.
